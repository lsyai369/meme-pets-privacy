Meme Pets — Support

Hand-drawn desktop pets for your Mac

Meme Pets puts little hand-drawn animals on your desktop — a Maine Coon cat, a Bichon Frise, and a Malayan night heron. They wander, nap, dance, and react when you play with them.

Getting started

  • Meme Pets runs from the menu bar (a 🐾 paw icon at the top-right of your screen) — it has no main window.
  • Click the 🐾 icon → Add Pet to place a pet on your desktop.
  • Open Pet Settings… from the same menu to resize pets, toggle Gravity, or generate food files.

Playing with your pets

  • Feed: drag an image file onto a pet. Each pet has a favorite treat (the wrong food makes it grumpy).
  • Interact: click, double-click, or drag a pet around for different reactions.
  • Dock: right-click a pet (or the Dock icon) to send it into the Dock.
  • Hide: the 🐾 menu can hide all pets for 30 min / 1 hr / 3 hr — handy during meetings. They return on their own.

FAQ

The app won't open on first launch ("cannot verify")

Right-click the app → Open → Open again. This is only needed the first time.

I don't see my pet

It may be behind a window or near a screen edge. Add another from the 🐾 menu, or open Pet Settings to check your pets.

How do I remove a pet?

Right-click the pet → Remove This Pet, or use Pet Settings.

Meme Pets — 支援

Mac 上的手繪桌面寵物

Meme Pets 讓手繪小動物住進你的桌面 —— 緬因貓、比熊犬,還有黑冠麻鷺。牠們會 到處走、打盹、跳舞,還會在你和牠互動時做出反應。

開始使用

  • Meme Pets 從選單列運作(螢幕右上角的 🐾 腳印圖示),沒有 主視窗。
  • 點 🐾 圖示 → Add Pet 就能在桌面放一隻寵物。
  • 同一個選單的 Pet Settings… 可以調整大小、開關重力、產生 食物檔。

和寵物互動

  • 餵食:把圖片檔拖到寵物身上。每隻都有最愛的食物(餵錯會 臭臉)。
  • 互動:點一下、點兩下、或拖著牠移動,都有不同反應。
  • 放進 Dock:右鍵寵物(或 Dock 圖示)可把牠收進 Dock。
  • 隱藏:🐾 選單可把所有寵物隱藏 30 分鐘 / 1 小時 / 3 小時, 開會很方便,時間到會自己回來。

常見問題

第一次打開說「無法驗證開發者」

右鍵 App → 打開 → 再按一次打開。只有第一次 需要這樣。

找不到我的寵物

可能被視窗擋住或跑到螢幕邊緣。可從 🐾 選單再加一隻,或開 Pet Settings 查看。

怎麼移除寵物?

右鍵寵物 → Remove This Pet,或用 Pet Settings。
